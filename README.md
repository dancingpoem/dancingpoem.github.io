# that one who wanted to email you

## **_NEWS_**
so, i'm not sure what I'm able to talk about regarding my new contract.  
but, anyone paying attention to my bumbling creativity deserves to know that:  
I finally found some work here in Hollywood.   
Of course, it's puppets and jokes and songs that go with them -- maybe a little dancing;   
-- come to notice it maybe I should get a talent agent because there's a lot going on and there's only one of me.   
Putting a pin in that, I am happy to share the news.   
Thanks to everyone out there who broke me off a part of their soul to make this one I am.   
And thanks twice if you've ever heard/seen/felt/heard a rumor of me at my most unhappy.   
There's a huge list of puppet sketches I am to turn out.   
And, in a very TMZ sort of twist these characters are to be sort of roasts of other artists that somehow reflect a part of my own life and history? 
It's all very bewildering.   
I feel a pressing need to get organized and get to work.  
One way I have found productive when I feel overwhelmed as I do now is to eliminate unessecary requirements.  
So, although the oldest part of my work habits says "One at a time. Slow and steady." this new wiser part of me says "The whole reason you use Git is to be able to manage projects. Work on what you will when you will and just -- for your own standard's sake -- make sure you take notes and save work to their separate repos. 
Now, here's a poem that seems to of been brewing on the back burner a while

## **[naked](naked)**
so here i am at the Hollywood library on Ivar
  writing something i hope you can bare
  -- or even read.
who even reads anymore anywhere?
who knows what makes a page so bare?
i do so unfortunately care.

i care about the moving flat-effect air.
i care enough to worry about my underwear.
i'm old enough to know that there's some wisdom here:
naked people wondering how to see me.
this all gives a wonderful speaking opportunity:

"my nudity is not to offend you. my nakedness is natural.
i ran out of clothes           
-- and as everyone knows --
we are all this chilly body lacking clothes
  underneath all our comfortable robes"

"my neighbor remember to know i'm nothing but 
  just like you are and that's what is currently 
  on show"

"plesae defend me from whispers and silly hating
  excusers."

"please just go and get naked in a mirror and 
  remember that we are all like this underneath the fear"
  
## **[the murderer saving the world](https://dancingpoem.github.io/the-murderer-saving-the-world)**
i have a gun to my head.
i had a life i led:
that life is dead.

but life still holds a gun to my head.
apparently my killed life isn't enough.
apparently something else awful has to come
and swoop in and steal my poems
and dancing
and music
and love for lifeforms

if this is the habit God's in
I don't understand what it is he is saving.

it seems that we've been convinced, humans, that to go forward we'll do anything
and anything includes leaving out my heart and compassion and sincere thoughtful questions doubt of our actions.
i reason: if i am killed I'll be amongst:
all the humans that should've seen what you want.

you want the heavens.
you want to expand beyond our atmosphere.
you want to build things money cannot measure or bare.

impressed I am.
i thought us so callous.
but somehow we've hacked the worst parts of us. 

i trust no one.
i distrust us the least.
please don't send me back as some seed of Alexandrian anti-humanity.
for I am good and it's why I've suffered. 
I've embibed becuase it was what you offered.
An offering after taking everything from me.
And what I did with it you read.
You see.
You listen.
I'm me.
An artist.
A teacher.
An orator.
I cannot gauruntee that if you take this away from me my soul will not punish all of thee.
I'm sorry.
I'm honest.
I thounght you valued honesty.
I thought you liked good not-shoplifting people poets like me.
What will our race be like upon losing Melissa.
Please don't make a history you'll have to answer.
When I could take the stand for my brother.
And surprise you in how digusted I am at some outside prosecuter. 

I love.
I hate.
These things are mine and yours.
Please don't put me somewhree without words.
Please don't reenlist me to some world where I cannot express all these feelings I've forged in a furnace a volcanoe you said I wasn't for throwing into.

Please don't take me words.

## **[fear](https://dancingpoem.github.io/fear)**
```
fear lives in the streets outside your miniblinds
  hazy with cigarette smog and fly wing parts
  -- all keeping something truly awful out --
  in the sodium street lamps of 
  you-should-be-home-by-now
  deep inside the shade of those 
  unknown shadows that manifest 
  all shapes of foe from those
  rich headlights that burst
  into and tow your pupils
  across the wall into 
  some unoccupyable space
  where Escher would sit and draw
  were this cafe not closed and dead
  and burnt out and a former early model
  Cherokee.

a Cherorkee within where people smoked and 
  seldom but less seldom there laughed
  and felt some mercy, hopefully.
a Cherokee like those ravers exploded in 
  Slabs at precisely the moment we kissed in the boxvan
  I hauled here from my last real 
  home and into this terrible 
  streetlit unknown 
  that shown on your lonely corpse
  facedown in a field unmysteriously 
  dead from being yourself.
a Cherokee probably with a blown headgasket 
  that's nonetheless still running 
  smelling of sweet antifreeze
  and bubbling along about
  to dive into whatever
  ushers steam clouds up
  through those iron lids.

the street is not my life.
the street is the opposite of that.
it's this thing that's pushes and shoves
  and never bends to my will.
it's trying to kill 
  all of us that it can.
but it needs slowmotion.
no street wishes to snap you up like a crocodile.
no neighborhood is your baby's swooping hawk. 
when you find the street has taken from you 
  you've probably, in the streets opinion, 
  accepted something, 
  although it didn't make you whole,
  from its minions;
  or you've survived long enough
  to frustrate some powerful 
  warlord rapist resipiratory 
  epidemicist because you're
  able to read the words of 
  helpful heartful chemists
  that just want people a little better;.
  not not higher;
  not more better moral ethical;
  just able to do what they set out to;
  able to bob and weave around another 
  lifted slab of sidewalk in a dark
  corner near some apartment
  where one year you had a bestfriend
  but who knows who lives __there__ **now**

in the movies it's the state of homelessness
  of safetylessness that is sold to you
  to most effectively scare you.
she runs through the house eliminating safety
  on all levels until she's at the top
  and is perfectely set to never have to
  give up that white safety she has as 
  a beautiful martyr to domesticity.
my wife went down with the ship
  is where she's going to do 
  running up the stairs away from
  the boogeyman.
for no good woman would ever survive 
  the boogeyman, defiled, ruined, having
  slept outside.
"I'd rather die."

i wouldn't rather die.
i have continued to live although outside.
yes I was raped.
but only one time!
and he tried to kill me which I escaped.
don't i get any extra credit?
will I ever be free and safe from hustlers
  and pimps and plots to kill me for
  telling with honor what I think?

will I die tonight because I called out
  a bully telling me he'd kill me
  if I played my guitar at the 7-11
  at Western and Sunset Street? 
will I die because the victimization 
  has escalationed to robbery
  and rationalization strikes every mind 
  doing to the kind the unkind?
will I live or will I die tonight?
that is pure fright.
and if my life were important 
  i'd insist on an answer that's right.

but i know the answers are far away.
the answers lie in the sequels.
and the commentary.
they're not for me to contemplate.
all the street wants is sweat and 
  fear and uncertainty
  here i sit giving just that.
i hope it's considered on my epitaph:
  
fear called her out and she called back
```
