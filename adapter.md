
# they've won

i'm writing a poem thousands of miles from anything like a place i can call home.  
they've won.  
the common it-having man has been pushed out.  
chased out!  
egad!  
chased out of every nook and cranny and room-you-rent at auntie's.  

we litter the streets.
easy pickings.
clearly problematic.
yes.
clearly a problem.
yes.
clearly the problem.
yes.
the only problem.

the design on a final outreach solution to the issue of my existence.
and they will have their way.
free love lost a long time ago.
for whom i even write poetry, i do not know.

# popcorn 
### **title-first study #1**

o, can i ever know anything as perfectly nostalgically calorically-meaningless thing
	as plain popcorn.
nothing is the flavor of this feed.
only toppings can help it.
but toppings eaten alone are often better.
don't give me that "butter"!
such an easy mantra.
bam! splat! wick wat! 
wiffleball bat!
this is it.

this is where i'm at:
like ice at the soda fountain,
let's do away with the popcorn.
buckets of powdered butter and cheese and caramal solid salt-lick-looking caramel.
o, can i ever know anything like popcorn
